# T2.2a: Review the recorded Equinox SOC evidence

## Goal

Use the two committed, redacted 2024 Equinox spike recordings to verify only the OBDb state-of-charge signals that agree with the recorded 70% dashboard observation. The public Equinox replay then prints those two readings as `verified`, retains the three cell-voltage readings as `community`, and cites the recording in a vehicle-specific evidence map. Prepare an attributed draft correction to OBDb's 2024 support filters; publishing it is a later owner-directed step. T2.2b covers a new car session and the remaining signal review.

## Non-goals

- Inferring battery health, capacity, or HV pack voltage; `DA1D/33E5` returned `NO DATA` and its OBDb maximum is 25.5 V.
- Calling a cell-voltage signal verified because its ordering or magnitude looks plausible without an independent comparison or a recorded state change.
- Changing the pinned, vendored OBDb `default.json` or `LICENSE`; changing the importer default from `community`; modifying the phone console, guard, relay, or recordings.
- Filing an issue or pull request, pushing a branch, or editing the shared plan. The draft is a reviewable proposal, not an upstream submission.

## Interfaces

Existing `importObdbMode22(input)` continues to return every signal as `community`. Add a bounded Equinox-specific evidence overlay in pure TypeScript:

```ts
export interface EquinoxSignalEvidence {
  readonly recording: string; // committed *.redacted.jsonl relative path
  readonly check: string;     // observation and plausibility rule, without a health claim
}
export const equinoxEv2024Evidence: Readonly<Record<string, EquinoxSignalEvidence>>;
export function withEquinoxEv2024Evidence(
  imported: readonly ObdbMode22Signal[],
): readonly ObdbMode22Signal[];
```

The map has exactly `EQUINOXEV_SOC_HD` and `EQUINOXEV_SOC`, with the first spike recording path and its 70% dash note. The overlay returns fresh signal objects, promotes only those IDs **when their module/DID also match `CB/27C6` or `CB/2B43`**, and preserves imported definitions and all other tiers. It does not accept external or user-supplied evidence, and does not alter the OBDb formulas. `scripts/replay.ts` applies it only when recording meta identifies `chevrolet-equinox-ev-2024`; other cars and the public importer retain their prior behavior. A replay line's `tier=verified` must come from this cited overlay, never from a positive ECU reply alone. The evidence map is the vehicle profile's signal provenance for T2.2; keep its paths readable by future profile/report consumers.

## Risks / open questions

- Largest risk: mistaking a dashboard's rounded 70% display for independent high-resolution truth. Verify only agreement at display precision; T2.2b must check a second SOC and may downgrade either signal.
- The 2025 OBDb `dbgfilter` conflicts with both 2024 positive replies, but a proposal to include 2024 is a support correction, not evidence that all 2024 vehicles or model years respond. Keep the draft conditional and cite recording lines.
- Dash does not show cell average/min/max. The three cell readings stay `community` until a second recorded state and an independent check establish plausibility.
- Upstream PR publication remains open after the draft; no external write is part of this spec.

## Decisions

- Use the two committed redacted spikes for a narrow 70% SOC agreement check; a new recording is required for the broader cell-voltage review. This preserves the distinction between vehicle model-year evidence and signal-level verification.
