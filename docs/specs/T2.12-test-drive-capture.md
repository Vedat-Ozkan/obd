# T2.12: Test-drive feasibility capture

## Goal

ADR-022 adds a test-drive check: effective pack resistance from current steps (BM8) and cell groups that sag under load (BM3). A 20-minute drive cannot give capacity (ADR-022, Why). T2.12 only finds out whether the drive check can be built. It records one drive on the Equinox and answers three questions:
1. How often can the phone read pack current `17`/`2414` and pack voltage `17`/`2885` on their own, and how often when the group voltages are read too?
2. How far apart in time are a group-voltage reading and the current samples around it?
3. Are acceleration and regen current steps large and clean enough for ΔV/ΔI?

The capture is a Developer tools button, **Record test drive**, on the check screen. The owner taps it once while parked in Ready and then drives. It needs no screen interaction until it stops by itself: at 20 minutes, or about a minute after the car is switched off. It runs in the existing foreground service, saves to the remembered capture folder, and is read-only.

A new script, `pnpm drive-capture <recording>`, turns the recording into the measured intervals, the skew, and a list of steps with ΔV/ΔI and their line numbers.

Test-visible outcome:
- The E2E test runs `runDriveCapture` against a fake ELM and passes the saved file through `driveCaptureSummary`. The summary recovers the fake's known resistance, and the fast current interval is shorter than the interval across a group read.
- The owner's drive then produces:
  - a redacted recording;
  - `docs/test-drive-2026-09.md`, with the measured intervals, at least one worked ΔV/ΔI example cited to recording lines, and a GO / GO (resistance only) / NO-GO verdict for T2.13.

## Non-goals

- Any resistance estimate, error band, temperature normalisation, group-sag flag or report. Those are T2.13, scoped by these findings.
- A third check mode in the navigation (CarScreen, `intent`), or a user-facing screen. That is T2.13.
- Changing the charge log's behaviour, policy values or stop rule (`chargeLogger.ts` changes only in which names it exports), `obd-battery/src/session.ts`, or `obd-core`.
- New DIDs, Mode 01 speed or ambient reads, or a temperature scaling. `2AF1` stays raw (T2.4 §Sources, Gaps).
- Beta upload of the drive recording (no new `queueForBeta` kind), and the deferred share sheet (see Design, Safety).
- A laptop script (see Design, Why in the app).
- Fixing the charge log's own timeout/link-loss replay marker (Risks). Edits to `docs/PLAN.md` or `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` (orchestrator only).

## Design

**Why in the app, not a laptop script.** The first question is about the phone's rate: the Android BLE stack, the notification latency and the JS loop that T2.13 will run. A bleak script on a laptop would measure a different stack, and it would put a running laptop in the car. The app already has everything a long read-only run needs:
- the foreground service (ADR-021);
- streaming save to the capture folder (T2.4 B2);
- session recovery (T2.4 Decisions 16–22);
- the kept link;
- the one-and-done rule.

The owner prefers one-tap in-app flows, and T2.13 is in-app.

**Schedule (one block, repeated until stop).** Each block runs, in one `CycleSession`:
1. `scanMode22Profile(session, DRIVE_FAST_PROFILE)` (`17`: `2414`, `2885`, with the recorded setup).
2. `FAST_PAIRS − 1` more bare pairs, `22 2414` then `22 2885`, with no setup. The header stays `DA17F1`.
3. `scanMode22Profile(session, DRIVE_GROUP_PROFILE)` (`CB`: `2AE1`–`2AE7`, `2AF5`, `2B43`, `2AF1`).

There is no sleep between blocks: the achievable rate is what is being measured.
- The bare pairs measure the rate with no per-read setup. That is the best T2.13 could do for current and voltage alone.
- The current samples on either side of step 3 measure the rate when every cycle also reads the groups, and they bracket each group set, which answers question 2.
- `2AF5` (BMS min/max and their record indices) is included so that the findings can say whether one read could stand in for seven group reads in T2.13.

`FAST_PAIRS = 10`. From T2.4's recorded reply times (single reads 68–82 ms, AT 20–40 ms), a block should take about 1.6 s of fast reads plus about 1.2 s of group reads. That puts a group set every about 3 s, which covers a sustained acceleration, with hundreds of samples of each kind in 20 minutes. It is a policy value, not a vehicle constant.

**Stop rule.** The run stops at the first of:
- `DRIVE_S = 1200` s since the run started, reason `"20 min recorded"`. This bounds the file (about 2 MB) and matches the owner's 15–20 min;
- `DRIVE_SILENT_S = 60` s with no decoded current sample, reason `"no current for 60 s (car off or not in Ready)"`;
- Disconnect, reason `"disconnect pressed"` (as for the charge log);
- a non-link error, reason = the message (T2.4 Decision 16).

Why these rules:
- A "current near zero for N minutes" rule was rejected. In Ready the pack draws about 1 A when parked and in traffic alike (docs/discovery-2026-09.md §7.4), so it cannot tell parked from a long red light.
- Switching the car off is the unambiguous end. Module `17` then stops answering: the 2026-09-28 car-off charge logs got only `UNABLE TO CONNECT` (`fixtures/recordings/chevrolet-equinox-ev-2024/2026-09-28-charge-log*.jsonl`, local, per the orchestrator). Current stops, so the silent rule ends the run within about a minute.
- Stops are checked after every pair and after the group block.
- A car not in Ready at start produces no current, so it stops after 60 s with that reason.

**Recovery, flushing and the file.** These copy `runChargeLog` exactly and share its code:
- `CycleSession`, `ReplyError`, `LinkError`, `StopRequested`, `reasonFor` and `clock` become exports of `chargeLogger.ts`. Adding `export` is that file's only change.
- `FLUSH_S` and `RECOVERY_WAIT_S` are already exported.
- The recorded link view (`open`, about 15 lines) is copied, not extracted. Extracting it would edit T2.4's run loop.
- Each session starts with `recording.meta({ event: "charge-log session", reason })`. That is the boundary record `chargeLogFromRecording` splits on (T2.4 Decision 9), so the drive replays through the existing function unchanged. The first meta line's note names the test drive.
- Every fresh session writes `ATZ`/`ATI` first (docs/ELM327.md §Write safety).
- After a failure that is an `ElmSessionError` timeout or a `LinkError`, the runner writes `recording.meta("timeout waiting for '>' (<message>)")` before the next tx. That tx has no `>`, and `packages/obd-core/test/recordings.test.ts` (`timedOut`) needs this marker to replay a committed recording.
- The run ends with the meta line `test drive stopped: <reason>`, a final flush, `copyToFolder`, and the status line `Test drive stopped: <reason>. <saved>`.

**App wiring.**
- `runtime.ts`:
  - The file-creation body becomes a local `createCapture(stem)`. `phoneTargets.create` calls it with `"charge-log"` (unchanged behaviour).
  - New export: `driveTargets: StreamTargets`, whose `create` uses `"test-drive"`. The file is `captures/<date>-test-drive[-N].jsonl`.
  - `foregroundService.start` takes optional `title`/`desc`, defaulting to today's charge-log strings.
- `chargeRun.ts`: the one module-level long-run record is shared. `ChargeRun` gains `readonly kind: "charge" | "drive"`, and `begin(connection, line, kind = "charge")`. Sharing the record keeps everything that already works for the charge log:
  - the synchronous `bleBusy()` gate;
  - ignoring BLE loss while a run owns the link;
  - Disconnect as `requestStop`;
  - surviving an unmount, the final line on remount, and the Back lock.

  A second record would need each of these guards duplicated.
- `ConsoleScreen.tsx`:
  - A `testDrive()` handler beside `chargeLog()`. It takes the same start steps (notification permission, `phoneTargets.folder()` resolved now, the mounted/link re-check, then `chargeRun.begin(active, "Starting the test drive…", "drive")`) and runs `runDriveCapture` in `foregroundService.start(task, "Test drive running", "Starting the test drive.")`. It connects through `runView` and `dongleLink.reconnect()` as in `chargeLog()`, and releases the same way.
  - The meta note is `T2.12 test drive: <note>`.
  - Nothing is queued for beta, and `deferShare` is not called.
  - `chargeLog()` itself is not edited.
- Render, with `driving = chargeLogging && chargeRun.current()?.kind === "drive"`:
  - title "Test drive";
  - the charge-step card is hidden while driving;
  - the running card says: "Recording a test drive. It stops and saves by itself after 20 minutes, or a minute after you switch the car off. Do not touch the phone while driving. Disconnect stops it early; what was recorded is kept.";
  - Disconnect requests the stop with "Stopping the test drive after the current command…".
- Developer tools: a **Record test drive** button, gated exactly like Start charge log (`canUseEquinoxConsole`, connection, non-empty note, not busy), with the caption "Car in Ready, parked. Tap once, then drive 15–20 min."
- Running status: `Test drive: m:ss of 20:00 recorded; <n> current samples.`, updated after each block. The notification shows the same text, at most every `NOTIFICATION_INTERVAL_MS`. Recovery lines reuse the charge log's wording.

**Safety.** The only tap is the start, made while parked. The capture folder is resolved before the run begins, so no picker opens at the end. Nothing opens a share sheet or an alert during or after the run. If the folder copy fails, the final line names the private file, and the owner pulls it with `adb exec-out run-as <package> cat files/captures/<name>` (the dev client is debuggable). Every command is `genericProfile` init or `scanMode22Profile`/`22 <DID>` through `Elm327Session`'s guard (hard rule 5).

**Analysis script.** `packages/obd-battery/scripts/drive-capture.ts` replays through `chargeLogFromRecording`, so decoding stays T2.4's. It prints markdown with exactly these parts:
- **Header**: `Recording:`, `Synthetic:`, `Stop:` (the `test drive stopped` note), `Span:` (first to last tx, s), `Sessions:` (the count, with each non-start reason and its t).
- **`## Intervals`**: n, median, p90 and max (s, nearest rank) for:
  - current *fast*: consecutive `2414` samples in one session with no `22 2AE1` tx between them;
  - current *across a group read*;
  - voltage fast;
  - voltage across a group read;
  - the current→voltage offset within a pair;
  - complete group sets: the count and the dropped count; their interval; and the span from the `2AE1` tx to the `2AE7` tx.
- **`## Skew`**, per complete group set:
  - *before*: the `2AE1` tx t minus the previous `2414` tx t;
  - *after*: the next `2414` tx t minus the `2AE7` tx t;
  - *bracket*: before + span + after.

  Each has its median, p90 and max, plus the share of sets whose two bracketing currents differ by ≤ 10 A.
- **`## Command times`**: the median time from a tx to the next tx, for AT commands, `22` on `17` and `22` on `CB`.
- **`## Steps`**: consecutive *fast* current samples a→b with |ΔI| ≥ `STEP_MIN_A`. Each current sample is paired with the `2885` sample from its own pair. R = −ΔV/ΔI in mΩ. The section gives:
  - the count, and R's median and quartiles;
  - a table of the 10 largest |ΔI|: t, Δt, I a→b, V a→b, R, and the 1-based line numbers of the four tx lines (2414 a, 2885 a, 2414 b, 2885 b).
- **`## Context`**: SOC (`2B43`) first and last, `2AF1` raw hex first and last, current min and max.

`STEP_MIN_A = 20`. It is a policy value, just under the smallest step BM8 names, the ≈ 26 A charger step (docs/ML.md §Methods).

**GO / NO-GO rule for T2.13** (applied in the findings note; the owner confirms, as for Gate B):
- G1: fast current median interval ≤ 1.0 s. BM8 uses fixed 2–10 s windows (docs/ML.md BM8), so this gives at least 2 samples in the shortest window.
- G2: at least 20 steps with |ΔI| ≥ 20 A in the drive.
- G3: median R > 0, and the interquartile range ≤ 50 % of the median. There is no sourced reference resistance, so this tests plausibility and consistency, not a match to a known value.
- G4: median bracket ≤ 2.0 s.

The verdict:
- G1–G4 all pass: **GO**.
- G1–G3 pass and G4 fails: **GO (resistance only)**. T2.13 then drops group flags or tries `2AF5`.
- Otherwise: **NO-GO**, with the failing criteria named.

## Risks / open questions

- **Dev-mode JS off Metro.** The dev client has not yet been shown to keep running after Metro is gone. If H2 dies when Wi-Fi drops, the fallback is an EAS `preview` build, as in T2.4's open question 6. That is an owner step and a native build, so it is out of this spec. Dev-mode JS may also slow the loop slightly, so the findings label their intervals "dev client".
- **Pair skew inside a step.** `2414` and `2885` are read one after the other, tens of ms apart. A step that lands between them gives a wrong R. The desk tests do not exercise this: D1's fake answers `2885` from the current of the same pair, so skew first shows on the real drive, in the G3 spread. The findings report the spread honestly (G3), and T2.13 can then decide on filtering.
- **The existing charge log has the same replay gap.** A charge-log recording with a timeout or link loss has no `timeout waiting for '>'` marker, so a committed copy would fail `recordings.test.ts`. That is noted for a follow-up, not fixed here (hard rule 6).
- **Group sets may not complete while driving** (bus load). The dropped count in the summary shows it, and G4 or T2.13 scope follows from it.
- **Open question (1): resolved**, see Decisions 1.
